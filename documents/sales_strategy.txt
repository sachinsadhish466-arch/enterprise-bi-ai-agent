COMPANY SALES STRATEGY

1. Revenue Growth
The company aims to increase quarterly revenue by 15%. Sales teams should prioritize profitable products and focus on retaining existing enterprise customers.

2. Regional Strategy
The South region should strengthen enterprise relationships and expand opportunities in technology and energy. The West region should focus on manufacturing and finance customers. The North region should develop healthcare and technology accounts.

3. Discount Policy
Sales representatives may offer discounts up to 5% without manager approval. Discounts above 5% require approval from the regional sales manager. Discounts above 10% require approval from the sales director.

4. Customer Retention
Account managers should contact strategic enterprise customers every month. Customers with declining purchase volumes should receive a business review and a retention plan.

5. Profitability
Revenue growth must not be pursued at the expense of sustainable profitability. Sales teams should review product margins before offering discounts.

6. Performance Review
Regional sales performance should be reviewed monthly using revenue, profit, customer retention, and order growth. The management team should investigate substantial negative changes before recommending corrective action.
